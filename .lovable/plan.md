## Plan: Sharpen "Who Benefits from the Cycle"

### What we're doing
Remove the redundant softening language in the "Who Benefits from the Cycle" section and call out the industry directly. The current text repeatedly qualifies that "not every" actor is bad — this undercuts the mission when the systemic problem is the norm, not the exception.

### Changes

#### 1. Intro paragraphs (lines 1019-1021)
**Current:**
- "Not every attorney is predatory. Not every consultant started with bad intentions. Not every clinician providing documentation is acting unethically."
- "But good intentions do not automatically create good incentives."
- "We need to be willing to look directly at the business models the current system has produced."

**Rewrite:**
- Remove all three softening sentences.
- Replace with a single, direct opening: acknowledge the industry is built on veteran confusion, then name the business models without hedging.

#### 2. Card body copy (lines 117, 125, 133)
Tighten each card to remove hedging and passive phrasing.

**Card 1 — VA Accredited Attorneys:**
- Remove "The legal structure may be permitted. The incentive structure still deserves scrutiny."
- State the conflict directly: back-pay-linked fees create a perverse incentive to delay, not resolve.

**Card 2 — Paid Strategists:**
- Strengthen from "The problem is not helping veterans understand the system" to direct callout: these programs sell confusion back to veterans as a product.
- Remove the cautious "creative" scare-quotes — name what they are.

**Card 3 — Nexus Letter Factories:**
- Remove "ValorWell is not pretending otherwise. Veterans may need legitimate clinical documentation. Nexus-related documentation can matter."
- State plainly: one-visit, one-document, no-follow-up-care models are not clinical relationships — they are transactions that leave veterans stranded.

#### 3. Pull quotes (lines 118, 126, 134)
Sharpen all three for punch and directness.

**Card 1 pull:** Replace "Legal does not automatically mean well-designed..." with something that names the actual incentive problem.

**Card 2 pull:** Replace "The harder the system is to understand..." with a direct accusation: the business model *depends* on confusion.

**Card 3 pull:** "Documentation can be part of the answer..." — remove "can be." The pull should state that retail documentation is part of the problem, full stop.

#### 4. Guardrail (line 1046)
**Current:** "ValorWell may support Nexus-related and other clinical documentation when it is clinically appropriate and connected to real care. The enemy is not the document. It is the transaction-first model and the broader workaround cycle."

**Rewrite:** Remove the hedging "may support" and "The enemy is not the document." The guardrail should state our position cleanly without apologizing for it: we do clinical documentation as part of care; we do not do document-first retail. The enemy *is* the transaction model.
